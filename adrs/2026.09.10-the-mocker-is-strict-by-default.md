# The mocker is strict by default

Date: 2026.09.10

Status: Accepted

## Context

pyreqwest's `client_mocker` passes an unmatched request through to the real network, and lothc's first draft copied that the same day.

## Decision

`lothc_mocker` raises `AssertionError` on an unmatched request. Opt out with `@pytest.mark.lothc_mocker(strict=False)` or `.strict(enabled=False)`.

## Consequences

A test can't silently reach the network.
