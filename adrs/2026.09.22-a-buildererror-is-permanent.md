# A `BuilderError` is permanent

Date: 2026.09.22

Status: Accepted

## Context

`sse()` spent its whole reconnect budget (15s) on a request that could never be built.

## Decision

A builder error is never retried or reconnected. A `RedirectError` still is, since a redirect loop can be transient.

## Consequences

A bad URL or a scheme refused by `https_only` fails at once.
