# Response headers are a `CaseInsensitiveDict`, not a `dict`

Date: 2026.09.22

Status: Accepted

## Context

`dict(raw_response.headers)` made `["Content-Type"]` raise and dropped repeated values.

## Decision

A `MutableMapping`, not a `dict` subclass (which would silently lose case-insensitivity under `dict(h)`), as in requests, niquests, httpx and httpx2. `get_all` returns every value.

## Consequences

Equality with a plain mapping compares only the single-valued view, so it's non-transitive with repeated headers, as in requests.
