# The sync and async clients mirror each other

Date: 2026.08.18

Status: Accepted

## Context

`HTTPClient` and `SyncHTTPClient` would otherwise drift as features are added to one.

## Decision

Implement every feature on both, with the same methods and overload shapes. Async names are bare and sync ones `Sync`-prefixed (`AuthProvider`/`SyncAuthProvider`).

## Consequences

`tests/test_sync_async_parity.py` (from 2026.09.23) compares their signatures and bodies, with reasoned allowlists, so a change to one client fails until its twin matches.
