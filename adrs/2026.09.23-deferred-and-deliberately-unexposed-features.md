# What's deferred, and what's deliberately not exposed

Date: 2026.09.23

Status: Accepted

## Context

A code review suggested more features than anyone had asked for.

## Decision

`sse_post` is deferred by the user. Hooks, a generic `request()`, streaming uploads, status/headers on the streaming verbs and cookie-jar access wait until someone asks. Per-algorithm compression toggles, `interface`, `tcp_nodelay`, `referer` and CRLs aren't exposed.

## Consequences

There's no final URL on `Response` either, since pyreqwest's response has none.
