# JSON-object forms decode with stdlib `json`

Date: 2026.10.23

Status: Accepted

## Context

This replaced [Bare `dict` replaces `lothc.JSON`, with its own overload](./2026.08.24-bare-dict-replaces-lothc-json.md), which still rejected `dict[str, Any]`.

## Decision

`dict`, `dict[str, Any]`, `typing.Dict[str, Any]` and `Mapping[str, Any]` decode with stdlib `json`, no extra needed. Bare `dict` keeps its own overload, typed `type[dict[Any, Any]]`, since `dict[str, Any]` made zuban reveal `Any`.

## Consequences

Every parametrized `dict[K, V]` also lands on that overload and types as `dict[str, Any]`, though it decodes, validated, through a library.
