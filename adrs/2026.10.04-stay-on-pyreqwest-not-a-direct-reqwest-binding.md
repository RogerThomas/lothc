# Stay on pyreqwest, not a direct reqwest binding

Date: 2026.10.04

Status: Accepted

## Context

A one-call PyO3 binding to reqwest was prototyped to see whether lothc's own binding would be worth it.

## Decision

Stay on pyreqwest: the binding gained only 5-12% on async `get()`, nearly all of it from the binding rather than lothc's Python layer.

## Consequences

lothc ships no compiled code and keeps pyreqwest's sync, streaming, multipart, TLS, error taxonomy and mocking, which a binding would have to reimplement.
