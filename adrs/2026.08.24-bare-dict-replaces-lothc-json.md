# Bare `dict` replaces `lothc.JSON`, with its own overload

Date: 2026.08.24

Status: Superseded by [JSON-object forms decode with stdlib `json`](./2026.10.23-json-object-forms-decode-with-stdlib-json.md)

## Context

This replaced [`lothc.JSON` is the only plain-dict decode target](./2026.08.18-lothc-json-is-the-only-plain-dict-target.md): a plain `dict` does everything `JSON` did, with no lothc-specific name to learn.

## Decision

Remove `lothc.JSON`, and give a bare `dict` decode target its own overload. `dict[str, Any]` is still rejected.

## Consequences

`dict[str, Any]` still fails at runtime.
