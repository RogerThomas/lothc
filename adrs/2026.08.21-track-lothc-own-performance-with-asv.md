# Track lothc's own performance with asv

Date: 2026.08.21

Status: Accepted

## Context

`task perf` compares lothc with other clients at one point in time, not across lothc's history.

## Decision

asv (`asv.conf.json`, `asv_bench/`) benchmarks lothc across commits, and `task bench-check` measures an uncommitted working tree.

## Consequences

lothc has separate benchmark setups for separate questions: `task perf`, asv, and CodSpeed from 2026.10.21.
