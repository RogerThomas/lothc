# `into=str` means raw text, mirroring `bytes`

Date: 2026.10.23

Status: Accepted

## Context

`into=str` was JSON-parsed, so `sse(into=str)` failed on `data: hello`.

## Decision

`str` is the body as strict UTF-8, SSE `data` as is, and each streamed line as text.

## Consequences

Bad bytes are a `UnicodeDecodeError`, which is still a `ValueError`.
