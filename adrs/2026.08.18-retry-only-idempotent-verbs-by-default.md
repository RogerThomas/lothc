# Retry only idempotent verbs by default

Date: 2026.08.18

Status: Accepted

## Context

A blind retry of a POST or PATCH can apply it twice.

## Decision

`max_retries` retries GET, PUT, DELETE and HEAD (and OPTIONS from 2026.11.05) on 429/500/502/503/504, through pyreqwest's `with_middleware`. `retry_methods` opts other verbs in, and `backoff_base` (public from 2026.09.22) tunes the delay.

## Consequences

A POST is never replayed unless the caller asks. The middleware wraps `send()`, so it can't retry a stream once its headers have arrived.
