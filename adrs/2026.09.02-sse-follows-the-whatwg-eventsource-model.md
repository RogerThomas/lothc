# `sse()` follows the WHATWG EventSource model

Date: 2026.09.02

Status: Accepted

## Context

Every SSE stream died at the client's `timeout`, and the retry middleware can't help, since `send()` returns once headers arrive.

## Decision

`sse()` defaults to a one-year timeout and reconnects itself per the WHATWG EventSource model: `Last-Event-ID`, `retry:`, until `max_reconnects` consecutive reconnects yield no event.

## Consequences

A stream runs until the server ends it or the budget runs out; `read_timeout` is the stall detector.
