# Other HTTP clients are benchmark comparisons only

Date: 2026.08.20

Status: Accepted

## Context

Not recorded.

## Decision

`perf.py` (`task perf`) benchmarks lothc against aiohttp, httpx, httpx2, niquests, aiosonic and requests. They're development dependencies, never runtime ones (from 2026.08.21).

## Consequences

Nothing in `lothc/` or `tests/` imports them, and since 2026.10.23 they live in their own `perf` dependency group.
