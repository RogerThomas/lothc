# Transport errors shadow the builtin exception names

Date: 2026.08.18

Status: Reversed by [Exceptions are `HTTP`-prefixed](./2026.08.21-exceptions-are-http-prefixed.md)

## Context

requests and httpx name theirs the same way.

## Decision

lothc's errors are `TransportError`, `TimeoutError` and `ConnectionError`, deliberately shadowing the builtins.

## Consequences

A module that imports them loses the builtin names (`# noqa: A001`).
