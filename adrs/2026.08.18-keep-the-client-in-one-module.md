# Keep the client in one module until it earns a split

Date: 2026.08.18

Status: Accepted

## Context

Not recorded.

## Decision

Everything lives in `lothc/_client.py`, and `lothc/__init__.py` re-exports the public surface.

## Consequences

The module is long (pylint's `max-module-lines` was raised to fit). `lothc/_oauth.py` is separate only because it's built on the client.
