# Don't parallelize the test suite with pytest-xdist

Date: 2026.09.22

Status: Accepted

## Context

The suite was slow enough to consider running it in parallel.

## Decision

No pytest-xdist; the suite got faster by fixing its own waits instead.

## Consequences

`-n 4` saved under a second, for a new dependency, non-deterministic ordering and a degraded `-x`.
