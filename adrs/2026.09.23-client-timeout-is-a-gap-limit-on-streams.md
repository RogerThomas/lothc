# The client timeout is a gap limit on streaming verbs

Date: 2026.09.23

Status: Accepted

## Context

A total cap killed every healthy long stream at 30s.

## Decision

On `stream_get`/`stream_post`/`download`, the client `timeout` limits the gap between chunks, and a per-call `timeout=` is still a total cap. Sync enforces it by reading on a worker thread with a bounded queue.

## Consequences

A blocked sync read can't be cancelled, so a stalled one leaves its thread parked until the peer closes.
