# Deliver streamed bytes as they arrive

Date: 2026.08.27

Status: Accepted

## Context

pyreqwest's 64KB read buffer withheld a trickling SSE or NDJSON payload until the buffer filled or the stream closed.

## Decision

Set `streamed_read_buffer_limit(1)` on every streamed request, `download` included from 2026.09.23.

## Consequences

No measured cost: the socket still governs chunking.
