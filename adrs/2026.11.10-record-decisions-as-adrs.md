# Record decisions as ADRs

Date: 2026.11.10

Status: Accepted

## Context

[`CLAUDE.md` holding decisions and their reasons](./2026.11.10-claude-md-holds-decisions-and-reasons-only.md)
still loaded every decision into every session, though one only matters when the behaviour it
covers changes.

## Decision

Each decision is a record in `adrs/`, named `YYYY.MM.DD-slug.md` by the day it was made, indexed
in `adrs/README.md`, backfilled from the project's history. `CLAUDE.md` keeps what every session
needs and points here.

## Consequences

An accepted record isn't rewritten: a new one supersedes it. Read the relevant record before
changing the behaviour it covers.
