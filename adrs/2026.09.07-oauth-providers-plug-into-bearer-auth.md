# OAuth providers plug into `bearer_auth`

Date: 2026.09.07

Status: Accepted

## Context

OAuth 2 client credentials needs a token request before a client's own requests, and again on expiry.

## Decision

`OAuthProvider`/`SyncOAuthProvider` live in `lothc/_oauth.py`, open a short-lived `HTTPClient` per token request, and are plain `bearer_auth` callables. A `client_factory`, not a `timeout` parameter, configures that client.

## Consequences

The clients know nothing about OAuth, and `functools.partial(HTTPClient, ...)` covers the token endpoint's timeout, proxy and TLS.
