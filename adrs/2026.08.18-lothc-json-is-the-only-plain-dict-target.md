# `lothc.JSON` is the only plain-dict decode target

Date: 2026.08.18

Status: Superseded by [Bare `dict` replaces `lothc.JSON`, with its own overload](./2026.08.24-bare-dict-replaces-lothc-json.md)

## Context

Not recorded.

## Decision

`lothc.JSON`, a `dict[str, Any]` subclass, is the plain-dict target. Bare `dict` and `dict[str, Any]` are rejected at runtime.

## Consequences

Callers learn a lothc-specific name to get a `dict`.
