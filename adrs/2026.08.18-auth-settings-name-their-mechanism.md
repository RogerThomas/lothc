# Auth settings name their mechanism, at most one per client

Date: 2026.08.18

Status: Accepted

## Context

The earlier generic `auth_token`/`auth` names hid that both only ever produce a Bearer `Authorization` header.

## Decision

`bearer_token` (static) or `bearer_auth` (a callable), and setting both is an error. `basic_auth` joined as a third on 2026.08.24.

## Consequences

A fourth mechanism gets its own equally specific name.
