# `download()` exists to cut peak memory

Date: 2026.08.21

Status: Accepted

## Context

`get()`'s `bytes` path holds about three copies of a body at peak.

## Decision

`download()` streams into one buffer, or with `dest=` to a file, written atomically via a hidden sibling and a rename.

## Consequences

About two-thirds of `get()`'s peak in memory, and O(chunk size) to a file. `get()` stays as is: for the small bodies lothc targets, the copies are free.
