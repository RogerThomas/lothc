# `stream_get`/`stream_post` yield raw chunks, or NDJSON lines

Date: 2026.08.20

Status: Accepted

## Context

The roadmap planned a single `stream()` yielding raw chunks.

## Decision

Two verbs, `stream_get` and `stream_post`, yield raw chunks by default and decode each NDJSON line when given a decode target.

## Consequences

The decode target on these verbs applies per line, not to the whole body.
