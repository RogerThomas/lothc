# Drop `TypedDict` and typeguard decoding

Date: 2026.08.24

Status: Accepted

## Context

This reversed [Decode into a `TypedDict`, validated by typeguard when installed](./2026.08.18-decode-typeddict-validated-by-typeguard.md): typeguard's pure-Python walks were ~3.5x slower than the compiled validators, decode-only, and added no capability pydantic and msgspec lack.

## Decision

Remove `TypedDict` as a decode target and typeguard as an extra.

## Consequences

lothc has two optional decode libraries, pydantic and msgspec.
