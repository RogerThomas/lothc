# Today's pyreqwest behaviour is the contract

Date: 2026.11.10

Status: Accepted

## Context

An audit found 65 user-facing behaviours with no test, and none of 9 deliberate breakages failed the suite.

## Decision

Tests pin today's pyreqwest-transport behaviour through the public clients, async and sync, down to the exact bytes on the wire (`tests/test_wire_format.py`).

## Consequences

A golden value is regenerated only for a deliberate change.
