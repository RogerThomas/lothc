# Pre-1.0, `feat` bumps the patch and a breaking change the minor

Date: 2026.09.28

Status: Accepted

## Context

release-please needs a rule for bumping a 0.x version.

## Decision

`bump-patch-for-minor-pre-major` and `bump-minor-pre-major` in `release-please-config.json`.

## Consequences

A breaking change still moves the minor (0.1.0 and 0.2.0 came that way), and 1.0 needs a `Release-As:` footer.
