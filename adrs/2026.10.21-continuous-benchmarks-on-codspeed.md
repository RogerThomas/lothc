# Continuous benchmarks on CodSpeed, through the mocker

Date: 2026.10.21

Status: Accepted

## Context

Benchmarks over a real network aren't deterministic.

## Decision

`benchmarks/codspeed/` runs on every push to `main` and every PR in CodSpeed's CPU simulation mode, with every request through `lothc_mocker`, so no socket is opened.

## Consequences

It measures lothc's own request-building and decoding overhead, not the network, and lives outside `testpaths`.
