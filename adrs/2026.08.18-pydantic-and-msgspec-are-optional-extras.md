# pydantic and msgspec are optional extras

Date: 2026.08.18

Status: Accepted

## Context

lothc decodes into, and encodes from, models of libraries a caller may or may not use.

## Decision

pydantic and msgspec (and at first typeguard) are optional extras, and lothc must work with none, either or both installed. Their imports are isolated in `lothc/_compat.py`.

## Consequences

Every use of either library is guarded: `import lothc` never fails for a missing extra, and a decode target that needs one is a `TypeError` naming it.
