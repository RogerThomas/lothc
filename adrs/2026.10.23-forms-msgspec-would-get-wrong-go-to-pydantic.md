# Forms msgspec would decode wrongly go to pydantic

Date: 2026.10.23

Status: Accepted

## Context

msgspec ignores pydantic constraints (`list[Annotated[int, Field(gt=0)]]` returned `[-5]`), and builds decoders for types it then can't decode, after the request was sent.

## Decision

A form with a pydantic marker, or one msgspec can't decode (a custom type such as `Path` or `OrderedDict[str, int]`), goes to pydantic; without pydantic it's a `TypeError` before sending.

## Consequences

Constraints are never silently skipped, and a form mixing a `Struct` with a pydantic marker is a `TypeError`.
