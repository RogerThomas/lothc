# A multipart `form=` request is sent once, never retried or replayed

Date: 2026.11.10

Status: Accepted

## Context

A multipart body is streamed, so it can't be cloned, and `form=` with retries failed before sending.

## Decision

Send it once, as reqwest's own retry policy treats a body it can't clone; a 401 still invalidates the token. Rejected: buffering the body, which means a second multipart encoder holding uploads in memory.

## Consequences

`form=` requests get no retries.
