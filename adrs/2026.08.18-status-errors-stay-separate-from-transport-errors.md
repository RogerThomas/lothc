# Status errors stay separate from transport errors

Date: 2026.08.18

Status: Accepted

## Context

A 4xx/5xx response and a request that got no response at all are different failures.

## Decision

`HTTPResponseError` (with a `body_start` snippet) and `HTTPTransportError` are separate classes with no common lothc base. Don't unify them.

## Consequences

A caller who wants both catches both.
